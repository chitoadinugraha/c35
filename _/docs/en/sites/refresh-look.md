# Refresh your site look
path: both

Use this when your public site still looks like an older template, or after Alien AI ships a guest renderer update.

## In chat (prompt)

Ask: **"Apply the modern storefront look to my site."**

You should see updated card radius, typography, and link layout in **Preview**. Then ask to **publish** so visitors get the same HTML.

## On the screen (editor)

1. Open your site in the app and go to **Design**.
2. On **Cards** or **Theme**, tap **Modern storefront**.
3. Open **Preview** and check the hub, products, and cart bar.
4. Tap **Publish** when you are ready for the live URL.

## After a platform renderer update

Alien AI sometimes bumps the **guest renderer** (compiled public HTML). You do not edit that number yourself.

| What you did | What visitors see |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Only saved a draft | Old published page until you **Publish** |
| **Published** after editing theme | New theme on the live site |
| Renderer bumped on the cluster | Stale HTML until first visit recompiles, or you **Publish** |

Most published sites refresh on the **first visit** after a renderer bump. Owners can also **Publish** to force the latest compile immediately.
