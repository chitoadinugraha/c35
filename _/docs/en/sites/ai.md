# Storefront AI Assistant and Live Voice Call
path: both

Give your storefront visitors an intelligent AI assistant that answers questions, helps find products, takes voice calls, and manages orders.

## What it does

Visitors on your published site or draft preview can interact with your storefront assistant via text chat or live voice call. When enabled, visitors can tap the voice call button on the storefront to speak directly with your AI assistant, inquire about products and pricing, check availability, and add items to their shopping cart hands-free in real time.

## In chat (prompt)

Ask: **"Enable AI assistant and live voice call on my store."**

The assistant will update your site's AI settings, enable live voice calls, configure the welcome greetings, and remind you to publish.

## On the screen (editor)

1. Open **Sites** in the app and select your storefront.
2. Go to **AI Assistant** settings.
3. Turn on **AI Assistant** and toggle **Voice Live Call** on.
4. Set optional welcome messages for **Chat Intro** and **Call Intro**.
5. Tap **Publish** so visitors on your live website can see the voice call button and speak with your store.
