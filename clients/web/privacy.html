Communications: Chat, Voice, Video, and Push-to-Talk

Alien AI includes real-time messaging and calling features. This section describes how We handle data when You use chat, voice calls, video calls, push-to-talk (PTT), and related voice input.

Chat

Messages, attachments, reactions, and related metadata You send in chats are stored on Our servers so We can deliver them to recipients and sync them across Your devices. When You invoke AI features inside a conversation, message content may be processed by Our AI systems to generate replies or actions on Your behalf. Team and personal chats are isolated; We do not mix private conversation data across scopes.

Voice and Video Calls

Audio and video during calls are transmitted in real time using encrypted WebRTC connections through Our infrastructure partners (for example, LiveKit). We do not permanently record call audio or video unless You explicitly use a feature that saves a recording or You choose to share call content in chat. Call signaling metadata (such as participants, start time, and duration) may be retained for service operation, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention.

Voice Input and Speech-to-Text

When You speak to the assistant or use speech-to-text, audio may be captured on Your device or sent to speech recognition services to produce text transcripts. Transcripts are used to fulfill Your requests and may be stored as part of Your conversation history when You send them in chat. Our in-app speech page (/tts.html) runs in a WebView and communicates transcripts only to the Alien AI app on Your device.

Push-to-Talk (PTT)

When You hold the PTT button, short audio clips are captured from Your microphone and transmitted to recipients in real time. PTT audio is not stored on Our servers unless it is saved as a voice message or attachment in chat. PTT may require background microphone access on desktop platforms; You can revoke this permission in Your device or system settings.

You can manage microphone, camera, and notification permissions in Your device settings. Denying these permissions limits chat media, voice, video, and PTT functionality but does not affect text-only use of the Service.

Files and profile media

Photos, documents, and other files You upload are stored on Our content servers using content-addressed storage. Uploads are associated with Your account when You are signed in. Public file URLs may be shared in chat or displayed on Your profile; do not upload sensitive material You do not intend to share within the Service.

AI processing

When You use assistant, automation, or other AI features, We may send prompts, conversation context, and attachments to third-party model providers to generate responses or actions. We use this processing only to operate features You request. Do not submit information You are not permitted to share with service providers.

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies remain on Your personal computer or mobile device when You go offline, while Session Cookies are deleted as soon as You close Your web browser.
